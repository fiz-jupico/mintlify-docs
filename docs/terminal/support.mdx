Jupico Terminal is the iPhone app Jupico merchants use to take in-person payments with Apple Pay. This page answers the common questions. If yours isn't here, email **support@jupico.com** and we'll reply within one business day. For a question about a payment, include the transaction ID shown in the app.

## Getting started

- **Who can use it:** Jupico merchants whose account has **Mobile Terminal** turned on. If it's off, the app says "Mobile Terminal is off". Ask Jupico support to enable it, then log in again.
- **What you need:** an iPhone with iOS 16 or later, and your Jupico backoffice email and password. There is no separate sign-up.
- **Your customer** pays with Apple Pay, from their iPhone or Apple Watch. Physical card entry and Tap to Pay are coming in a later release.

## Logging in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your credentials">
    Enter your backoffice email and password, then tap **Log in**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the phone">
    The first time you log in on a phone, we email you a 6-digit **verification code**. Enter it and tap **Verify**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stay remembered">
    That phone is then remembered for **15 days**, counted again from each login, so you won't be asked for a code each time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You stay logged in for up to 12 hours. After that, the app asks you to log in again.

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| Email or password is incorrect. | Use the same details as backoffice. If you forgot your password, contact Jupico support. |
| Too many failed attempts. Try again in 15 minutes. | After 5 wrong passwords, logins are paused for 15 minutes for your safety. |
| That code is not correct. | Check the latest email from Jupico and type the code again. |
| This code has expired. Please log in again. | Codes last 10 minutes. Log in again to get a new one. |
| Too many wrong codes. Please log in again. | Log in again to get a new code. |
| Your session has ended. Please log in again. | Your 12 hours are up, or you were logged out. Log in again. |

## Taking a payment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter the amount">
    Type the amount on the keypad. Use **⌫** to delete a digit, or press and hold it to clear the amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a note (optional)">
    Add a **note**, for example "Table 4". It appears in backoffice next to the payment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Charge">
    Tap **Charge**, then hand the phone to your customer to pay with Apple Pay.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm approval">
    The payment is complete when the app shows **Payment approved** with a transaction ID. Tap **New sale** to take the next one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The maximum amount is $999,999.99.

| Message | What it means |
|---|---|
| The payment was declined. | The customer's bank refused it. Ask them to try another card in their Wallet. You can tap Apple Pay again on the same screen. |
| We couldn't confirm whether this payment went through. | The connection dropped while the payment was processing. **Don't charge the customer again** until you check the payment in backoffice. |
| Payments are unavailable right now. | Jupico couldn't be reached. Wait a moment and tap **Try again**. If it keeps happening, contact support. |
| This charge cannot be paid again. | The sale was already paid or is being checked. Start a new sale if needed. |
| Mobile Terminal is off. | Mobile Terminal isn't enabled for your account. Contact support. |

## After the payment

- **Where to find it:** every payment appears in your Jupico backoffice reporting, marked as a **Mobile Terminal** sale, with the amount, note, and transaction ID.
- **Refunds:** refund from backoffice, as for any other payment.
- **Receipts:** the app shows the approval on screen. Email receipts aren't available in the app yet.

## Security and privacy

- Card numbers never reach the phone or the app. Apple Pay sends a single-use payment token, which Jupico processes.
- Your login is stored in the iPhone's Keychain, only on that device. Tap **Log out** to remove it. Logging out keeps the phone remembered for the 15-day verification window.
- No advertising or tracking. See the [Privacy Policy](https://terminal.jupico.com/privacy).
- To delete your account or its data, email support@jupico.com. Merchant accounts are managed by Jupico, not inside the app.
