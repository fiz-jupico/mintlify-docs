Jupico Terminal lets Jupico merchants take payments from their iPhone.

Log in with the same user you use for Jupico backoffice, confirm with a verification code, and start selling. Enter the amount, add an optional note, and complete the payment with Apple Pay. The result shows right away, and every payment appears in your Jupico backoffice reporting, marked as a **Mobile Terminal** sale.

## Features

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Secure login" icon="lock">
    Two-factor verification with a code sent to your email. Your iPhone is remembered for 15 days.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Simple keypad" icon="calculator">
    Enter the amount and add an optional note, such as "Table 4".
  </Card>
  <Card title="Apple Pay" icon="apple">
    Your customer pays with Apple Pay from their iPhone or Apple Watch. Jupico processes the payment.
  </Card>
  <Card title="No stored card numbers" icon="shield-check">
    Card numbers never reach the phone. Apple Pay sends a single-use token only.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in">
    Use your Jupico backoffice email and password. There is no separate sign-up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your phone">
    Enter the 6-digit code we email you the first time you log in on a phone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Charge">
    Type the amount, tap **Charge**, and hand the phone to your customer to pay with Apple Pay.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track it in backoffice">
    The approval shows on screen with a transaction ID. The payment appears in your backoffice reporting, where you can also refund it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Requirements

- A Jupico merchant account with **Mobile Terminal** enabled. Contact Jupico support to turn it on.
- An iPhone with iOS 16 or later.

Jupico Terminal is free. It has no advertising or tracking. See the [Privacy Policy](https://terminal.jupico.com/privacy).
